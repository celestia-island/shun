# The drive is full

The install ran out of room while writing files. Windows stops the moment the target drive is full, even halfway through a copy.

- Free some space, or pick another drive in the installer.
- Windows: Settings → System → Storage. macOS: System Settings → General → Storage. Linux: your distro's disk tool.

Leave at least a gigabyte free: unpacking keeps a second copy of the files while it works, and the download cache takes room too.

[Windows reference: free up drive space](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/experience/storage-filemanagement/free-up-drive-space-in-windows)
